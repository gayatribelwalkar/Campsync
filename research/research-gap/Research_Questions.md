# Research Questions and Hypotheses

## Research Questions

The proposed CampSync system aims to answer the following research questions.

### RQ1

How can an offline-first healthcare management system improve patient registration and medical data collection in NGO healthcare camps operating in low-bandwidth environments?

---

### RQ2
How does integrating medical equipment inventory management with patient healthcare records improve resource utilization and equipment tracking?

---

### RQ3

Can automated WhatsApp-based follow-up improve patient engagement and healthcare continuity after medical camps?

---

### RQ4

How does a unified platform connecting field volunteers, NGO administrators, and patients improve operational efficiency compared to existing fragmented systems?

---

### RQ5

Can real-time analytics support better decision-making during NGO healthcare camp operations?

---

# Research Hypotheses

### H1

The use of an IndexedDB-based Progressive Web Application significantly reduces patient registration time compared to traditional paper-based registration.

---

### H2

Offline-first synchronization reduces data loss and improves healthcare record availability in low-connectivity environments.

---

### H3

Integrated medical equipment tracking decreases equipment misallocation and improves inventory management.

---

### H4

Automated WhatsApp follow-up increases patient response rates compared to manual follow-up methods.

---

### H5

A unified healthcare platform improves operational efficiency and coordination among field volunteers, NGO administrators, and patients.
