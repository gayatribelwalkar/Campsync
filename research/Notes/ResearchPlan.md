# Research Plan
## Project Title

CampSync: An Offline-First NGO Healthcare Camp, Patient and Medical Equipment Management System

---

# Research Objective

The objective of this research is to study existing healthcare camp management systems, NGO healthcare information systems, offline-first web applications, patient management systems, and medical equipment inventory systems. Based on this study, the project aims to identify existing limitations and propose CampSync as an integrated solution for NGOs conducting healthcare camps in low-bandwidth environments.

---

# Research Questions

1. How are healthcare camps currently managed by NGOs?

2. What problems do NGOs face while managing patient records and medical equipment?

3. How do offline-first applications improve healthcare services?

4. What technologies are commonly used in healthcare management systems?

5. How can patient follow-up be automated?

6. What research gap exists in existing systems?

7. How does CampSync address these limitations?

---

# Research Scope

This research focuses on:

- Healthcare Camp Management
- Offline-First Web Applications
- Patient Record Management
- Medical Equipment Inventory
- Hospital Referral Management
- WhatsApp-Based Patient Follow-up
- NGO Digital Transformation

---

# Expected Deliverables

- Literature Review
- Comparative Analysis
- Research Gap
- Proposed Architecture
- Software Engineering Documentation
- Research Paper

---

# Expected Contribution

CampSync proposes a unified platform that combines offline patient registration, automatic synchronization, inventory management, hospital referrals, and automated follow-up into a single system designed specifically for NGOs operating in areas with unreliable internet connectivity.
