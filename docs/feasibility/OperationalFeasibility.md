# Operational Feasibility

# CampSync: A Resilient, Low-Bandwidth Consolidated NGO Healthcare & Inventory System

---

# 1. Introduction

Operational feasibility evaluates whether the proposed system can be effectively used by its intended users in real-world environments. It focuses on user acceptance, ease of operation, required training, workflow integration, and the overall practicality of implementing the system.

CampSync is designed specifically for NGO healthcare camps operating in low-bandwidth and remote areas. The system has been planned by considering the daily activities of volunteers, administrators, and patients to ensure smooth adoption with minimal operational changes.

---

# 2. Target Users

The primary users of CampSync are:

- Field Volunteers
- NGO Administrators
- Patients

Each user interacts with the system according to their responsibilities.

---

# 3. Operational Feasibility for Field Volunteers

Field volunteers are responsible for patient registration, recording vital signs, updating medical records, and identifying patients requiring referrals or medical equipment.

### Operational Advantages

- Simple touch-friendly mobile interface
- Large buttons for outdoor use
- Minimal typing using dropdowns and checkboxes
- Works without internet connectivity
- Fast local patient search using IndexedDB
- Automatic synchronization when internet becomes available

The system significantly reduces paperwork and minimizes duplicate data entry during healthcare camps.

---

# 4. Operational Feasibility for NGO Administrators

NGO administrators manage camp operations, inventory, reports, and patient follow-ups.

### Operational Advantages

- Centralized web dashboard
- Real-time analytics
- Equipment allocation management
- Patient follow-up tracking
- Automated reporting
- Live synchronization status monitoring

The dashboard enables administrators to supervise multiple healthcare camps from a single interface.

---

# 5. Operational Feasibility for Patients

Patients benefit from improved communication and follow-up services.

### Operational Advantages

- Faster registration process
- Reduced waiting time
- Digital health records
- Automated WhatsApp notifications
- Easy feedback submission
- Improved continuity of care through referral tracking

Patients do not need technical knowledge to interact with the system, as communication is handled through familiar messaging platforms.

---

# 6. Training Requirements

The system requires minimal training because it is designed with user-friendly interfaces.

Estimated training duration:

| User | Training Required |
|------|-------------------|
| Field Volunteers | 2–3 Hours |
| NGO Admin | 1 Day |
| Patients | No formal training required |

Training sessions should include:

- Patient registration
- Offline data entry
- Synchronization process
- Inventory allocation
- Dashboard navigation

---

# 7. Workflow Integration

CampSync integrates seamlessly into the existing NGO healthcare workflow.

Current manual activities such as paper-based registration, spreadsheet management, WhatsApp communication, and inventory tracking are consolidated into a single digital platform.

The transition requires minimal changes to existing operational procedures.

---

# 8. Ease of Use

The system is designed with usability as a primary objective.

Key usability features include:

- High-contrast interface
- Large touch controls
- Responsive design
- Fast navigation
- Clear status indicators
- Minimal data entry effort

These features improve efficiency during busy healthcare camps.

---

# 9. Reliability

CampSync remains functional even during complete internet outages.

Offline capabilities include:

- Patient registration
- Medical record entry
- Equipment allocation requests
- Local data storage
- Patient search

When connectivity is restored, synchronization occurs automatically without requiring user intervention.

---

# 10. Risk Analysis

Possible operational risks include:

- Initial resistance to adopting digital systems
- Incorrect data entry by volunteers
- Temporary synchronization delays
- Mobile device battery depletion
- Limited digital literacy among some users

These risks can be mitigated through proper training, intuitive interface design, and operational guidelines.

---

# 11. Operational Benefits

The implementation of CampSync provides several operational benefits:

### Tangible Benefits

- Faster patient registration
- Reduced paperwork
- Improved inventory management
- Reduced manual reporting effort
- Better patient follow-up tracking

### Intangible Benefits

- Increased volunteer confidence
- Improved patient satisfaction
- Better NGO coordination
- Enhanced transparency
- Improved decision-making through analytics

---

# 12. Conclusion

CampSync is operationally feasible because it aligns with the existing workflow of NGO healthcare camps while significantly improving efficiency, data accuracy, communication, and patient care.

The system requires minimal training, supports offline operation, and provides a user-friendly experience for volunteers, administrators, and patients.
