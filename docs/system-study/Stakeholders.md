# Stakeholders

## Primary Stakeholders

### NGO Administrator

Responsible for managing healthcare camps, volunteers, patient records, medical inventory, and organizational reports.

---

### Field Volunteer

Registers patients, records vital signs, collects medical information, and synchronizes offline data with the central server.

---

### Doctor

Reviews patient history, records diagnosis, prescribes medication, recommends hospital referrals, and requests medical equipment when necessary.

---

### Patient

Receives healthcare services, medical equipment, follow-up notifications, and healthcare recommendations.

---

## Secondary Stakeholders

### Equipment Manager

Manages donated medical equipment, allocation, maintenance, and inventory status.

---

### Hospital Staff

Receives referral information for patients requiring advanced treatment.

---

### NGO Management

Monitors organizational performance, camp statistics, healthcare impact, and resource utilization.

---

### Donors

Contribute medical equipment or financial support and may receive reports regarding equipment utilization and healthcare impact.
