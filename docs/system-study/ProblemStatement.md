# Problem Statement
# 4. Problem Context

Current NGO healthcare operations experience several challenges:

- Paper-based patient registration is time-consuming.
- Patient records are scattered across notebooks, spreadsheets, and messaging applications.
- Medical equipment allocation lacks centralized tracking.
- Volunteers struggle to update records in areas without internet access.
- Patient follow-up depends on manual phone calls or WhatsApp messages.
- Administrators have limited visibility into ongoing healthcare camps.
- Decision-making is delayed due to fragmented information.

These issues reduce operational efficiency and increase the possibility of data duplication, equipment loss, and missed patient follow-ups.

CampSync addresses these problems through an integrated offline-first digital healthcare ecosystem.

## Existing Situation

Many NGOs conducting healthcare camps continue to rely on manual processes such as paper-based patient registration, Google Sheets for maintaining records, separate WhatsApp groups for communication, and independent spreadsheets for equipment management. These disconnected workflows increase administrative workload and reduce operational efficiency.

During community service at Ravindra Joshi Medical Foundation (RJMF), it was observed that volunteers were required to perform multiple repetitive tasks including collecting patient information on paper, transferring records into spreadsheets, coordinating through WhatsApp groups, and manually tracking donated medical equipment. Since healthcare camps are often conducted in areas with poor internet connectivity, real-time cloud-based systems become difficult to use effectively.

The absence of an integrated platform makes patient follow-up, equipment allocation, inventory management, and historical record retrieval time-consuming and error-prone.

---

## Problem Identified

The existing system suffers from the following limitations:

- Paper-based patient registration.
- Duplicate data entry.
- Separate management of patients, equipment, and follow-ups.
- Lack of centralized patient history.
- Difficulty tracking donated medical equipment.
- Dependence on internet connectivity.
- Manual follow-up through phone calls and WhatsApp.
- Limited reporting and analytics.

---

## Need for the Proposed System

A centralized offline-first healthcare management platform is required to integrate patient registration, healthcare camp management, medical inventory, hospital referrals, and patient follow-up into a single system while allowing uninterrupted operation in low-bandwidth environments.

CampSync addresses these challenges by combining offline data collection, automatic synchronization, inventory management, and communication features within one platform.
