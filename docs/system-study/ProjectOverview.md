# Project Overview
## Project Title

CampSync: An Offline-First NGO Healthcare Camp, Patient and Medical Equipment Management System

---

## Introduction

CampSync is an offline-first web-based healthcare management system designed to support Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs) that organize community healthcare camps in rural and urban underserved areas. The system aims to digitize patient registration, medical record management, donated medical equipment tracking, inventory management, and patient follow-up activities within a single integrated platform.

The idea for CampSync originated from real-world observations made during community service at Ravindra Joshi Medical Foundation (RJMF). During the service period, various healthcare activities such as patient registration, medical equipment management, Google Sheets data entry, WhatsApp communication, and health camp organization were performed using separate tools and manual processes. This fragmented workflow increased administrative effort, created duplicate data entry, and made long-term patient tracking difficult.

CampSync proposes an integrated solution that allows field volunteers to continue working even in areas without internet connectivity. Patient records are stored locally using offline storage technologies and automatically synchronized with the central server once network connectivity becomes available. The system also supports inventory management, hospital referrals, and automated WhatsApp follow-up notifications.

The primary objective of CampSync is to improve operational efficiency, reduce paperwork, minimize duplicate data entry, and provide NGOs with a reliable digital platform for managing healthcare camps and patient services.

---

## Motivation

The motivation behind CampSync comes from firsthand experience gained during community service activities at RJMF. While volunteering, it was observed that healthcare camps involved multiple disconnected processes including paper-based registration, spreadsheet management, manual follow-up, and separate communication channels. These challenges highlighted the need for a centralized digital platform capable of functioning effectively even in low-bandwidth environments.

---

## Scope of the Project

CampSync focuses on digitizing the complete healthcare camp workflow including:

- Patient Registration
- Medical Record Management
- Health Camp Management
- Medical Equipment Inventory
- Hospital Referral Management
- Offline Data Collection
- Automatic Data Synchronization
- WhatsApp Follow-up
- Analytics Dashboard
- Report Generation

---

## Expected Outcome

The proposed system will enable NGOs to efficiently organize healthcare camps, maintain accurate patient records, track donated medical equipment, automate patient follow-ups, and improve decision-making through centralized data management while ensuring uninterrupted operation in areas with limited or no internet connectivity.
