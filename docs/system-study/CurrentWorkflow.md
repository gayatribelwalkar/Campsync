# Current Workflow (AS-IS System)

## Pre-Camp Activities

- Plan healthcare camp.
- Prepare registration sheets.
- Prepare medical equipment.
- Create WhatsApp groups.
- Assign volunteers.

---

## During Health Camp

Patient arrives

↓

Volunteer registers patient manually

↓

Vital signs are recorded

↓

Doctor consultation

↓

Medicine distribution

↓

Hospital referral (if required)

↓

Medical equipment recommendation

↓

Patient leaves

---

## After Camp

- Patient information transferred to Google Sheets.
- Equipment allocation maintained separately.
- Volunteers contact patients manually.
- Feedback collected using WhatsApp.
- Reports prepared manually.

---

## Limitations

- Paper-based workflow.
- Duplicate data entry.
- Multiple disconnected systems.
- Manual follow-up.
- Poor inventory tracking.
- No offline digital solution.
